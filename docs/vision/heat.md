# Vision: Heat, ICE and attention

Part of the long-term design target; index and status markers in `docs/VISION.md`.

## Heat, ICE and attention

- **Heat** = attention from authorities and rival criminals; the pressure to keep a low profile if you push too hard and make a name for yourself. Relevant **only during sleeve gameplay**. The Corporation phase gets a **similar but differently working** mechanic: everyone knows the company is acting, so personal "synth-level" heat no longer matters, but the player is harder to "attack" for it. OPEN: what the corporate version is.
- **TODO-RENAME:** the name "Heat" reads as a processor overheating. Consider something about attention/notoriety/exposure. Same for **Burn leads**, which are meant as burning bridges with contacts (giving them up and blaming them for what you did).
- **ICE** (Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics) = your shield against offensive hacking of you; it represents hiding from attention. **Leaning towards a change:** ICE simply makes heat cool down faster in general, so heat stays manageable at all times (instead of lowering the heat tax). Not final.
- **Heat in the corporation phase.** Sleeve heat **feeds a new stat on the corporation side**. It confers more of a negative effect on the **political** route than the underworld, and **ICE Rating is more of a need (and focus) in the political run**. This should fit thematically. Details OPEN.
