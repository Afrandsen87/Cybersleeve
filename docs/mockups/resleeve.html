  • Same chrome as the rest of the main screen: mono title with a status light on the right (LOCKED, a percentage, READY, CONFIRM), and a full-width mono button in the same shape as the Firmware INSTALL button. The big rounded sans-serif button goes.
  • 1–2 Unlock: the price is on the button. Affordable is the gold "can buy now" look used everywhere else; otherwise the button is dim and a line says how far off you are.
  • 3 Charging: the sleeve-level shortfall moves out of the tooltip into the box: a magenta progress bar (held / needed), the percentage in the title, and the need-and-have line. With more than one requirement, one line each and the bar follows the furthest-off one.
  • 4 Ready: full bar, green READY, and the button breathes magenta. The line says what you get on arrival.
  • 5 Armed: the long confirm sentence becomes a three-line ledger (RESETS / KEEPS / GAINS) inside a solid magenta box, with CONFIRM and CANCEL side by side. Same words as today's confirm text, just laid out. In the game the armed box expands upwards, not down: CONFIRM takes the RE-SLEEVE button's exact place and the ledger grows into the free space above it, so the buttons you click never move on screen.
  • Numbers are placeholders. All values come from what the button already reads (the buy view, resleeveRequirements, the confirm text); no new game data. The ledger lines and "on arrival" gain should come from the same text as the confirm message (one source).
