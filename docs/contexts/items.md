## Interface, state and config (S5, items half)

- **Inventory first-stack helpers (P12):** `Inventory.heldFirst / takeFirst / addFirst(itemId, ...)` work on the single stack an item without rarity keeps under the first rarity (Zero-days, Admin keys, Skill shards, their bonus drops, rarity-less drops). They replaced six hand-written `rarities[0]` lookups in `zeroDays.ts`, `skillShards.ts` and `drops.ts`. The `rarities[0]` lookups left are in `automation.ts` (Auto Burn: it needs the rarity id for `BurnLeads`) and `augments.ts` (takes by a chosen rarity).
- **`dropShare(rule, rarityId, firstRarityId)` (`items/drops.ts`, P12):** the chance one run drops a rule's item in a rarity (normalised weights). `DerivedStats.dropSources` sums it; real drops are split with the same weights.
- **Burn leads (P3):** `BurnLeads.use` delegates to `useMany(rarity, 1)`, so there is one heat formula (`heat x (1 - fraction)^k - flat x ...`). A single lead now gives the same heat as before up to the last floating-point digit (`burnLeads.test.ts` pins it to 1e-9).
- **Deferred to S5b:** moving the Zero-day shard bonus drop into `items` (it is now `perRunEvents` + `addFirst` in `crits/zeroDays.ts`); a `DropQueries` interface (the current `dropShare` function covers P12's share rule).
- **State / config:** unchanged (`inventory`, `items.*`).

## Design (from DESIGN.md)

### 10. Items, drops, the Inventory and Augments

- **Items** are an entity (`GameConfig.items`): kinds (Kompromat file, Augment schematic) in five **rarities**, most common first: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary, Crisis level. Counts are kept per kind and rarity in the **Inventory** widget (under Network, shown once a drop source is owned or something is held). **The widget is a grid of tiles:** every possible stack is a tile with the item's icon and its count underneath (a stack that is not held is dimmed), the border colour tells the rarities apart, and an item without rarity has one tile. The tiles are in two sections: items that are only kept or used on the **left**, items that can be sold in their own section on the **right**. **An item type is shown only once it is unlocked:** a purchase that drops it is owned, or some of it (any rarity) is held; every rarity of an unlocked item shows, even at 0. The inventory resets by re-sleeving (ASSUMPTION).
- **Drops** are random but computed per batch of runs (binomial and multinomial draws), never run by run, so a long tick and many short ones give the same *rate*. The chance and the rarity weights are data on the buyable's effect.
- **Kompromat is blackmail, not a sale.** In the section for sale a held tile is **selected by clicking it** (click again to deselect; an empty tile cannot be selected), and the buttons beside the grid act on the selected stack: **Sell** sells one, **Sell all** sells the rest (a stack that is sold out is deselected). The selection is a choice of the screen and is not saved. One file pays **100 credits** (common) and three orders of magnitude more per rarity (100,000, 100,000,000, 100,000,000,000, 100,000,000,000,000). A payment is flat: heat and the credit lever do not touch it, and the tip just says "flat". The tip says "Used as blackmail". The code calls it selling.
- **Rarity refinement.** Every whole order of magnitude of (effective) Hacking Proficiency, `floor(log10)` (none below 10), is one *shift*: **1% of each rarity's weight moves to the next rarity**, all amounts worked out first and then moved at once. With 90/9/1/0/0 the first shift gives 89.1/9.81/1.08/0.01/0, so only the second shift lets the last rarity drop. The last rarity keeps what it has; the total never changes. Hacking Proficiency resets by re-sleeving, so the shift starts over.
- **Every item tooltip shows the drop chance of its own stack**, as the chance that ONE completed run of an action drops it: the chance of the drop rule (with Nose for Secrets and the like) times the share of the rarity in the drop ratios as they stand now (Rarity refinement included), e.g. `Drop Chance = 0.45% per Practice Hacking run`. One line for each action that can drop the stack; `Drop Chance = 0%` while no owned buyable drops it, or while the rarity has no share yet. An item without rarity shows its whole chance.
- **Augment schematics** are applied, never sold (`sellable: false`: no *Sell* buttons, and the engine refuses). Every action card has an **Augment** button (top right) that applies **one** schematic to that action: **+3% to the action XP boost of ITS rarity**, additive within a rarity (3 common = +9%); the boosts of different rarities **multiply** (3 common, 3 uncommon and 3 rare give 1.09 × 1.09 × 1.09). Action XP only, never sleeve XP. You **select the rarity in the Inventory** (click its tile), then press the Augment button; only that rarity is used up. Applied augments are saved per action and rarity and reset by re-sleeving. Industrial Espionage drops them at 0.5% of runs with the rarity weights 90/9/1/0/0. The tip shows the calculation with the numbers filled in.
- **Burn leads** are dropped by Grow network (the Burn leads buyable, 0.75% of runs; the item has no rarity, so the Inventory shows one row named "Burn lead" and the drop names no rarity weights; internally the stack is kept under the most common rarity). They are not sold (`sellable: false`); there is no Sell for them: **clicking the Burn lead tile uses one** (the tile is disabled while there is no heat). One lead removes **5% of the CURRENT heat** (so it is worth more the hotter you are: 5% of 200 heat is 10, of 20 heat is 1) and is used up. The button is disabled while there is no heat, and a lead is never used up for nothing. The result is often a fraction of a point, which the cooldown then leaves alone (see 5). The share is data (`items.burnLead.heatFraction`). The inventory resets by re-sleeving like every other item.
- **Zero-days** are dropped by Push malware (the Zero-day exploits buyable, 5% of runs; no rarity, one stack, one row named "Zero-day"). They are not sold (`sellable: false`). **Clicking the Zero-day tile switches a toggle** (lit magenta while on); it cannot be switched on with none held. While it is on, **every completed Push malware run uses one Zero-day up and gets +100% critical chance** (a sure critical success, plus whatever the normal chance adds; see the Critical success buyable): a batch of runs is settled in one step, `min(runs, held)` of them covered. When the last Zero-day is used the toggle **switches itself off**; drops that arrive later do not switch it back on. Zero-days used up are not refunded when the run would be critical anyway. The toggle is saved (and read back as off when none is held) and resets by re-sleeving. Which action it works on is data (`items.zeroDay.actionId`).
- **Admin keys** are dropped by Kill servers (the Black box recorder buyable, 10% of runs; no rarity, one stack, one row named "Admin key"). They are not sold (`sellable: false`). **Clicking the Admin key tile switches a toggle** (the same mechanism as the Zero-day, with its own switch `state.adminKeysOn`, saved; it cannot be switched on with none held). While it is on, **every completed Practice Hacking run uses one Admin key up and gets +100% critical chance** (the usual critical rules), settled in closed form per batch: `min(runs, held)` runs are covered. When the last key is used the toggle switches itself off. **Skill shard drop:** every run an Admin key made critical (not one that was critical by chance, a Zero-day or a banked success) has a **2% chance** (placeholder, `items.adminKey.shard.chance`) to drop one **Skill shard**, drawn once per batch (binomial) and put in the Inventory.
- **Skill shards** have no rarity and are not sold. **Clicking the Skill shard tile uses one**: it lands on a **random unlocked action** (an action the access level allows; drawn through the injected random source) and **delays that action's XP scaling by 1 level**: leaving each of its first levels costs the first level's price, and the growth starts after them. With the default rule (10 XP, then x2): costs 10, 20, 40, 80 become 10, **10**, 20, 40 with one shard, and 10, 10, **10**, 20 with two. Each shard delays by one more level (`items.skillShard.levelsDelayed`). ACTION levels only; sleeve levels are not touched; it stacks with the XP reductions (Quick Study and the like scale the delayed thresholds). The count per action is saved and **resets by re-sleeving** (ASSUMPTION, like Augments).
- **Ransomware's bank** (`state.bankedCriticals`, per action): each critical success of Push malware adds one (+100% chance for one run); when Grow network completes runs, `min(runs, banked)` of them are covered (settled in closed form, like Zero-days) and the bank shrinks by that many. A success that the bank itself caused banks nothing unless that action has links of its own. The bank is saved (whole counts of known actions only) and resets by re-sleeving. Zero-days give Push malware's runs +100% chance, so together they feed Grow network.
- **Test drop** (test aid): drops one item by the current weights whatever the chance; before the harvest is owned it uses the configured rule.
- Known and accepted: selling stops mattering quickly because earnings outgrow it.

## Configuration (from docs/CONFIG.md)

### `items` – the item entity
| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `rarities[]` | `{id, name, sellValue}` | The rarity tiers, **most common first**. `sellValue` (BigNum >= 0) is what one item of the tier sells for in credits. Real content: Common 100, Uncommon 100,000, Rare 100,000,000, Legendary 100,000,000,000, Crisis level 100,000,000,000,000 (three orders of magnitude per tier) |
| `definitions[]` | `{id, name, sellable?, hasRarity?}` | The kinds of item. Every kind exists in every rarity unless `hasRarity: false`: such an item is one stack named by the item alone (the stack is kept under the first rarity), and a drop rule for it names no `rarityWeights` (the rarity of an item that has rarities needs them). Real content: `burn-lead` ("Burn lead", `sellable: false`, `hasRarity: false`), `kompromat` ("Kompromat file") and `augment-schematic` ("Augment schematic", `sellable: false`: it has no Sell buttons and cannot be sold; default `true`) |
| `augment?` | `{itemId, xpBonusPerItem > 0}` | Makes `itemId` the Augment schematic: the Augment button on every action card applies one of the rarity selected in the Inventory, adding `xpBonusPerItem` (0.03 = 3%) to the action XP boost of ITS rarity on that action, additively within a rarity; the boosts of the different rarities multiply (3 common, 3 uncommon and 3 rare give 1.03 x 1.03 x 1.03). Sleeve XP is not affected. Leave it out for a game without the button. Real content: `augment-schematic`, 0.03 |
| `zeroDay?` | `{itemId, actionId}` | Makes `itemId` the Zero-day: its Inventory tile becomes a **toggle** (it cannot be switched on with none held). While on, every completed run of `actionId` uses one up and is a critical success; the toggle switches itself off when none is left. Both ids are validated. Declare the item `sellable: false`. Leave it out for a game without the toggle. Real content: `zero-day`, Push malware |
| `adminKey?` | `{itemId, actionId, shard?: {itemId, chance in [0, 1]}}` | Makes `itemId` the Admin key: the same toggle as `zeroDay` (its own switch), for `actionId`. `shard` names an item **without rarity** that each run the key made critical drops with that `chance` (drawn once per batch). Declare both items `sellable: false`. Real content: `admin-key`, Practice Hacking, shard `skill-shard` at 0.02 |
| `skillShard?` | `{itemId, levelsDelayed >= 1 (integer)}` | Makes `itemId` the Skill shard: its Inventory tile is used by clicking it; it lands on a random unlocked action and delays that action's XP scaling by `levelsDelayed` levels (see `LevelRule.xpCostOfLevel`). Real content: `skill-shard`, 1 |
| `burnLead?` | `{itemId, heatFraction in (0, 1]}` | Makes `itemId` the Burn lead: its Inventory row gets a **Use** button that uses up one lead of that stack and removes `heatFraction` (0.05 = 5%) of the CURRENT heat. The button is disabled while there is no heat. Declare the item `sellable: false` so it cannot be paid out. Leave it out for a game without the button. Real content: `burn-lead`, 0.05 |

Ids must be unique, and items need at least one rarity. Selling pays `sellValue` per item in full: the heat tax and `global.creditMultiplier` do not touch it (they scale run rewards and passive income only).

## Decisions (from docs/DECISIONS.md)


*Group: Decided 2026-10-25 (by the user, items)*
- **Items are an entity** (`GameConfig.items`: kinds and rarity tiers with sell values). Practice Hacking gets the *Kompromat harvest* buyable (second on its card, 500 credits): 5% of completed runs drop a Kompromat file, rarity weights 90/9/1/0/0 over common, uncommon, rare, legendary, crisis. Items sell from the *Inventory* widget (under Network) for 10 credits at common and x10 per rarity. Drop chance and rarity weights are meant to change later, so they are data on the `itemDrop` effect, never constants in code. Random draws go through an injected `RandomSource` and are made per batch of runs (`core/kernel/sampling.ts`), never per run. Selling is not taxed by heat or scaled by the credit lever.

*Group: Decided 2026-10-25 (by the user, items)*
- **Augment schematics (2026-10-25):** *Industrial Espionage* (Grow network, 10,000 credits) lets Grow network drop the item *Augment schematic*. Every action card has an *Augment* button (top right) that applies one schematic: +1% to the action XP boost of ITS rarity, additively within a rarity, and the rarities multiply (3 common + 3 uncommon + 3 rare = 1.03 x 1.03 x 1.03); action levels only (not sleeve levels); the most common rarity held is used up first; schematics cannot be sold (`sellable: false`); reset by re-sleeving. The amount is data (`items.augment`).

*Group: Decided 2026-10-26 (by the user, Overclock, Focus and Burn leads)*
- **Burn leads:** a Grow network buyable (price is a placeholder, 1,000) lets runs drop the item *Burn lead* (5%; it has no rarity: one stack, shown as just "Burn lead", `ItemDefinition.hasRarity: false`). Using one (clicking its tile in the Inventory) takes 5% of the CURRENT heat and uses it up; leads are not sold (`sellable: false`). The share is data (`items.burnLead.heatFraction`).

*Group: Decided 2026-10-27 (by the user, Corporation and item tuning)*
- **Augments are applied by selecting a rarity in the Inventory**, then pressing an action's Augment button (`augmentAction(id, rarityId)` uses only that rarity; the selection is a choice of the screen, handed to the view model as `ViewSelection`, not saved). The Automation tab's dispersal still uses the most common rarity first. One Augment now adds **3%** (was 1%).

*Group: Decided 2026-10-27 (by the user, Corporation and item tuning)*
- **Drop and sale tuning:** Augment schematic and Burn lead drop chance **0.5%** (was 5%). Kompromat sells for **x1000 per rarity** (100, 100k, 100M, 100G, 100T; was x100): "an order of magnitude more" was read, after asking, as each step being 10x bigger.

*Group: Decided 2026-10-27 (by the user, new Anonymity skills)*
- **Silver Tongue** (the "lead hoarder" idea, renamed to something charismatic): reuses the existing `itemDropChanceMultiplier` effect (x1.5 on the Burn lead, so 0.5% becomes 0.75%).
