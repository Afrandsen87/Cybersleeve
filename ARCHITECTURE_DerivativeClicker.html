At a glance

No build step, no backend. One mutable global player object, saved to localStorage, drives everything.

~1,700lines of JS across 4 files
~50named functions in script.js
35buildings (7 tiers × 5 slots)
24reset-currency buyables, 31 achievements

Single global state

player plus small globals globalMult, numTiers, enableChart, isActive.

Imperative tick loop

update() reschedules itself with setTimeout; rendering re-templates and replaces innerHTML.

Two prestige layers

Tier resets (reset currency) and an infini-reset triggered when money overflows to Infinity.

File map & load order

All scripts share the global scope, so order matters.

data.js needs the constructors; script.js needs startPlayer and the templates already in the DOM; charts.js needs player and displayNum. Quirk: startPlayer reads versionNum before its var line runs (hoisted → undefined); versionControl repairs it.

Domain model

player.buildings[0..34]: index = 5 × tier + slot. Slot decides behaviour; hover a cell for detail.

0 Derivative: money (tier 1), feeds lower tier 1 Proof maker: money → proofs 2 Proof-cost building (×3 growth) 3 Clicker: money per click 4 Mathematician: tick speed and reset currency

Building record

Building(factor, moneyCost, proofCost) → {owned, manual, factor, moneyCost, proofCost}. owned includes generated units; manual counts only purchased ones and feeds calcMult. Cost grows geometrically; bulk price = price × (fn−1)/(f−1).

Reset-currency buyables (24)

4 columns × 6 tiers, each requires the one below it:
unlock tiers 5–7 & cost-factor cuts · click improvers · building-tick cuts · autoclick-tick cuts & conversion-factor cuts. Stored column-major, displayed row-major.

Multiplier formulas

mult[t] = 1 + tierUpgrades[t] × f[t] × Σ manual(tier t)   f = [.0005, .002, .005, .01, .02, .04, .06]
globalMult[t] = 1 + resetCurr[t] × 0.01
updateInterval = 1000 × 0.98^ln(mathematicians × mult[0] × globalMult[0] + 1)
