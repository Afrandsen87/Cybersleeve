AUTO BURN

A daemon that burns leads to keep your heat in check, and stops or restarts heat-making work.

  • Drag Skills owned to see the reveal order: each skill adds one row, notch or field, and nothing appears earlier.
  • The highlighted row is the rule acting right now. Move Heat and Burn leads to watch it change.
  • Floor and Redline are fixed numbers until Tune Thresholds; Reserve appears with Lead Reserve.
  • The status-line logic here is a rough mockup. The real panel reads it from the Auto Burn rules owner (autoBurnRules).
  • Icons are rough 16×16 sketches; final art goes in automationSkillIcons.ts. The heat scale 0–100 is only for the mockup.
