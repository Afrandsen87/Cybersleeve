# Anti-patterns (reject or rework)

- **Fake choices:** two options where one is always better. Check by asking what a player of any state would pick.
- **Dead upgrades:** a purchase that is never worth buying, or that is obsolete one minute after it unlocks.
- **Flat percentage buffs** ("+5% to everything") with no decision attached. Cybersleeve also forbids global multipliers.
- **A new layer that is only a multiplier** for the layer below.
- **Timers that punish idling** (life clocks, decay), unless that is the explicit design. Cybersleeve has no life clock.
- **Chores that grow with progress** (more things to click, more to babysit).
- **Hidden information** that the player must discover by wiki. Hide what is not yet relevant, but make what is shown legible.
- **Spoilers:** names or text that reveal later systems before they matter (see the reveal checklist).
- **Unbounded exponential growth in one lever** with no knee or cap, which collapses all other decisions.
- **"Just add another multiplier"** as a fix for a pacing problem. Find the wall and move it instead.
- **Per-run iteration** in logic that can be closed-form (breaks at astronomical scale).
- **Silent rebalancing** of existing tuning. Flag it with numbers.
